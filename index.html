What Xifona Does

Style intelligence, in your pocket.

Six tools built around one goal — help you look your best, every time you get dressed.

Outfit Recommendation

Not sure what to wear? Start here. Tell Xifona the occasion, your style, and the kind of look you're going for. Get outfit ideas that fit the moment, whether it's work, a date, a party, a casual day, or the gym.

CasualWorkDate NightFormalPartyGym

Visualize Your Look

See it before you wear it. Have an outfit in mind but can't picture how it will look on you? Visualize the complete look on yourself before you commit. Try different outfits, colors, and combinations without changing clothes ten times.

Improve My Look

Know what's working. Fix what's not. Take a photo of what you're wearing and get a clear breakdown of your fit, proportions, colors, and overall balance. Find out what could be changed and how to make the outfit work better for you.

Compare Outfits

Two looks. One easier decision. Can't decide between two outfits? Put them side by side and see which one works better for the occasion. Compare fit, color, proportions, coordination, and overall appearance to make the choice with confidence.

Advanced Analysis

Advice you'd expect from an experienced stylist. Go beyond a simple opinion. Get a detailed breakdown of your look, including color harmony, proportions, silhouette, fit, occasion suitability, styling details, and what to change. Think of it as having a second set of experienced eyes before you step outside.

Look History

Keep track of what works for you. Your analyzed and compared looks stay in one place. Revisit your outfits, compare past choices, and see how your personal style develops over time.
