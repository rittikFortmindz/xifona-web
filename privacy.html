This policy explains what information Xifona may collect, why it is used, and the choices you have around your photos, profile, and look history.

Information We Collect

  • Photos or image paths you capture, upload, preview, compare, or submit for AI analysis.
  • Profile details, style preferences, selected occasions, saved looks, scores, recommendations, and app activity.
  • Device, diagnostics, crash, network, and usage information used to keep the app reliable and secure.

How We Use Information

  • To provide outfit analysis, comparison, history, personalization, premium experiences, support, and app security.
  • To understand which features are working, improve AI quality, fix bugs, and prevent misuse.
  • To communicate important service updates, policy changes, and support responses when needed.

When Information Is Shared

  • We may share information with service providers that help run analysis, storage, analytics, payments, notifications, or support.
  • Payment details, if any, are handled by payment processors or app stores. Xifona does not store full card numbers.
  • We may disclose information if required by law, to protect rights and safety, or during a merger, acquisition, or business transfer.

Retention and Security

  • We keep information only as long as needed for the app, legal requirements, security, backups, or legitimate business purposes.
  • No app can guarantee perfect security, but we use reasonable safeguards designed to protect photos, preferences, and account data.
  • If you delete content or request deletion, some copies may remain for a limited time in backups, logs, or records required by law.

Your Choices

  • You can manage camera and photo permissions in your device settings.
  • You may request access, correction, export, or deletion of personal information where applicable.
  • You can stop using the app at any time, and you can contact support for privacy questions or account requests.

Age, Location, and Updates

  • Xifona is not intended for children under 13 or anyone below the minimum age required in their region.
  • Information may be processed in countries other than where you live, subject to appropriate safeguards where required.
  • We may update this policy as the app changes. The latest version will be shown in the app and on this page.

Terms of Use (EULA)
