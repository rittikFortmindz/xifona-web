This Privacy Policy explains how Xifona ("Xifona", "we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, discloses, and protects information when you use the Xifona mobile application, website, and related services (collectively, the "Services"). Please read it carefully before using the Services.

The entity responsible for your personal information is Xifona, located at Kolkata,WestBengal,India ("Xifona"). If Xifona is operated by an individual rather than a registered company, identify the individual or business name that legally operates the Services here.

Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on the features you use and the permissions you grant. It may include:

  • Photos and images: photos you capture, select, upload, or submit to analyze, compare, or visualize outfits, along with image files and related technical metadata where present.
  • Profile and preference information: information you choose to provide, such as your name or profile details, style preferences, selected occasions, and other personalization inputs. Only include profile fields that the app actually collects.
  • Generated content and app activity: outfit analysis results, scores, recommendations, generated or edited images, saved looks, credit usage, feature interactions, and related history.
  • Account and support information: account identifiers and information you provide when contacting support or making a privacy request.
  • Purchase and subscription information: subscription status, product identifier, transaction or entitlement information, trial eligibility/status where available, and purchase history information made available by Apple or our subscription service provider. We do not receive or store your full payment card number through Apple in-app purchases.
  • Device and diagnostic information: device type, operating system and app version, language, IP address or network information where processed, crash reports, diagnostic events, and usage information needed to operate, secure, and improve the Services.

We collect information you provide directly, information generated when you use the Services, and information received from service providers that help us operate the app.

Photos and AI Processing

When you submit a photo or prompt for an outfit-related feature, Xifona processes the submitted content to provide the feature you requested, such as outfit analysis, outfit comparison, styling recommendations, or outfit visualization.

  • Your submitted image and relevant inputs may be transmitted to Xifona's servers and to third-party infrastructure or AI service providers that process content on our behalf.
  • Analysis results, recommendations, and generated images may be returned to the app and may be stored so that features such as history or saved looks can work.

  • Apple handles payment-card details and payment processing for App Store purchases. Xifona does not receive the full payment-card number for those purchases.
  • RevenueCat helps Xifona validate and manage subscription entitlements, trial status, renewals, and purchase restoration. It may process transaction identifiers, product identifiers, subscription status, purchase dates, expiration information, and app user identifiers as configured.
  • Subscription price, trial length, renewal timing, and cancellation terms are shown in the app and/or Apple's purchase confirmation screens. You can manage or cancel an App Store subscription through your Apple Account subscription settings.
  • Deleting the Xifona app or requesting deletion of your Xifona account does not automatically cancel an active App Store subscription. Cancel the subscription separately through Apple.

For details about Apple's handling of purchase information, consult Apple's Privacy Policy.
